# Semiconductor Physics
Course Code: ECE000
- Course Category : PCM
- Course Type : IC-T
- L-T-P-X-C : 3-0-2-3-5

## Course description
A sample description

## Course Objectives
- To understand energy band theory in solids
- To analyze intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
- To study carrier transport phenomena

## Course Outcomes
- Explain energy band formation in solids
- Distinguish intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
- Analyze carrier transport mechanisms

## Unit 1: Semiconductor Fundamentals
Theory Hours: 9
- Energy band formation: E–k diagrams; conductors, semiconductors, insulators
- Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors: intrinsic semiconductors; extrinsic semiconductors; energy bands
- Doping, impurity ionization, and Fermi level: doping; impurity ionization; Fermi level
- Intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors: intrinsic semiconductors; extrinsic semiconductors; energy bands

Practical Hours: 6
- Experiment 1: PN Junction Characteristics
    - In this experiment, students will measure the I-V characteristics of a PN junction diode under forward and reverse bias conditions to determine the cut-in voltage and saturation current.

X Hours: 9
- X-Activity 1: Semiconductor Simulation
    - Students will use TCAD tools to simulate the energy band diagrams of various semiconductors.
    - The simulation results will be compared with theoretical E-k diagrams to validate the carrier transport models.
